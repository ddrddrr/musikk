\chapter{Introduction}\label{ch:intro}

Music streaming has been significantly growing in popularity
each year for the past decade.\parencite{music_stats_decade}
And this is to be expected; numerous studies showed how important
music is for people.
For instance, \citeauthor{music_role_life} in his 2012 paper shows
that it can influence mood, change self-perception, and help with social bonding.
In addition, the rise of the clubbing scene, music festivals,
and other social music-related activities clearly indicates the important social role of music.\parencite{concert_going_social_aspects}

Despite this, it is surprising that features that facilitate social interactions are not
widely implemented in the existing music streaming platforms, as will be shown in~\ref{ch:platforms}.

The goal of this thesis is to design a music-centric platform that provides capabilities for
collaboration and social interaction around music.
This work is divided into the following \textbf{six} chapters:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Chapter \ref{chap:survey}}
    Presents the outcomes of a survey illustrating how people consume music,
    how prevalent it is in social interactions, and why this type of platform can be relevant.

    \item \textbf{Chapter \ref{ch:platforms}}
    Explores what existing streaming platforms and other music-related services
    already offer.

    \item \textbf{Chapter \ref{chap:specification}}
    Outlines the functional and non-functional criteria for the application.

    \item \textbf{Chapter \ref{ch:planning}}
    Describes the choices of technologies that are used by the application.

    \item \textbf{Chapter \ref{ch:implementation}}
    Explains the development process and implementation details.

    \item \textbf{Chapter \ref{ch:conclusion}}
    Summarizes the results of the work.
\end{enumerate}
