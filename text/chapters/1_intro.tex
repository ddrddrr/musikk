\chapter{Introduction}\label{ch:intro}

Music plays an important role in everyday life.
\citeauthor{music_role_life}~\parencite{music_role_life} shows that music can
affect mood, change how people see themselves, and help them bond with
others. Music is also a social activity. People listen to it together at
clubs, festivals, and similar events.~\parencite{concert_going_social_aspects}
Over the past ten years, music streaming has grown a lot. It is now the
main way most people listen to music.~\parencite{music_stats_decade}

Even so, social features are not a main part of most streaming platforms,
as shown in \chapterref{ch:platforms}. No service today combines a personal
library, shared listening, direct messages, Comments on songs, and Friend
Activity in one place. A survey done for this thesis
(\chapterref{ch:survey}) shows that users want these two sides closer
together. Most people who answered said they would use more social features
on a music platform.

This bachelor's thesis aims to develop
a web-based music management and streaming platform focused on social interactions between users.
The platform combines a personal music library, audio Streaming, and social
features such as Chats, Comments, Feeds, and Follow relationships.

This work is divided into the following chapters:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \chapterref{ch:survey}
    Presents the outcomes of a survey illustrating how people consume music,
    how prevalent it is in social interactions, and why this type of platform can be relevant.

    \item \chapterref{ch:platforms}
    Explores which social features existing streaming platforms and other music-related services already offer.

    \item \chapterref{ch:specification}
    Defines the functional and non-functional requirements for the application.

    \item \chapterref{ch:planning}
    Describes the choices of technologies and architectural patterns used by the application.

    \item \chapterref{ch:implementation}
    Describes the development process and implementation details.

    \item \chapterref{ch:user_testing_improvements}
    Reports on user testing of the deployed application and lists known gaps and further improvements.

    \item \chapterref{ch:conclusion}
    Summarises the outcome of the work.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Conventions used in this thesis:}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Oversized figures} \\
    Some diagrams and survey charts in this thesis are too large to embed
    inline. They are referenced by their file path in the project
    repository, for example \path{text/diagrams/HighLevelSystem.png} or
    \path{text/charts/age.png}. The complete sets are kept under
    \path{text/diagrams/} and \path{text/charts/}.

    \item \textbf{Citations} \\
    Sources are cited on the first mention of a work, term, or claim.
    Later mentions of the same work or term are not re-cited.
\end{itemize}
