\chapter{Conclusion}\label{ch:conclusion}

The goal of this thesis was to design and build a music streaming
platform with social features. The motivation was based on the User
survey and on the review of existing platforms. Together, they showed
that music listening is often a social activity, and that no
current platform covers more than a part of the social features
looked at.

A web application was built as a result. It meets all ``Must Have''
and ``Should Have'' requirements from the specification, and brings
together social features that are otherwise split between several
existing platforms. Therefore, the main goal of the thesis was
achieved.

However, some features were outside the scope of this thesis, and some
issues are still not fixed, as shown in~\chapterref{ch:user_testing_improvements}.

To sum up, the final application fulfils the defined requirements, is
deployed and available to users, and therefore the thesis can be considered successful.
